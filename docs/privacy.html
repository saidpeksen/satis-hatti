Privacy notice

Last updated: November 2026.

What we collect

When you join the waitlist we store your email address, the date you joined and, if present, a short tag showing which link you came from (for example "tiktok"). We do not ask for your name, address or payment details, and this page does not use advertising or tracking cookies.

Why

Only to tell you when our product launches and to count how many people are interested. We will not sell or share your email for anyone else's marketing.

Where it is kept

Your email is stored on our own server. The page itself is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may log basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver the page.

How long

Until you ask us to remove it, or until we decide not to launch — in that case we delete the list.

Your choices

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your email at any time by writing to thepickedfew@gmail.com. Every email we send will include a way to unsubscribe.
